User personas are fictional representations of your target audience. They help teams align on who they're designing for, what their goals are, and how best to solve their problems.

## Why Personas Matter

Personas ground design decisions in real-world user behavior and expectations. They prevent design by assumption and keep user needs at the forefront.

### How to Create a Persona

1. Conduct user interviews and surveys to gather data.
2. Identify common patterns and group behaviors.
3. Create narratives with names, goals, frustrations, and backgrounds.
4. Validate personas with stakeholders and users when possible.

> "Empathy is not an add-on; it's the starting point of great UX." — Lana Wright

## Tips for Effective Personas

- Keep them realistic and grounded in data.
- Update them as your product evolves.
- Use them in every step of your design process.

Personas are a cornerstone of user-centered design. Start crafting yours today to shape better, more inclusive products.
